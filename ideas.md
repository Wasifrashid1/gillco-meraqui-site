# Gillco Meraqui — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Editorial Garden Modernism
Very Brief Intro: A calm, architectural luxury language grounded in landscape, generous whitespace, and magazine-like typography. It makes the project feel considered, not over-marketed.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Monsoon Bronze Minimal
Very Brief Intro: A darker, tactile direction using moody photography, bronze detailing, and gallery-like pacing. It leans cinematic and intimate.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Chandigarh Light & Line
Very Brief Intro: A bright, graphic direction inspired by modernist planning, clean grids, and botanical geometry. It feels open, rational, and optimistic.
Probability: 0.08

## Chosen Approach: Editorial Garden Modernism

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial architecture with traces of Indian modernism and biophilic hospitality design.

### Core Principles
1. Make landscape and architecture feel like one continuous composition.
2. Use asymmetry and editorial pacing instead of centered sales-page blocks.
3. Let cream, forest, and brass create quiet luxury without ornamental excess.
4. Treat floor plans as collectible documents: framed, legible, and navigable.

### Color Philosophy
Deep forest green carries the trust and permanence of the Gillco brand. Warm architectural cream creates light and calm, while muted brass adds a precise premium signal without turning the page flashy. A restrained clay accent echoes the supplied plans and warm façade imagery.

### Layout Paradigm
A layered editorial scroll: an image-led hero, staggered fact rail, offset project story, plan-document gallery, and a final conversion panel that feels like a private appointment card. Content should drift between full-bleed and narrow reading columns.

### Signature Elements
- Thin brass rules and corner brackets framing key project facts.
- Small uppercase wayfinding labels with generous letter spacing.
- Plan-card overlays that borrow from drawing-board annotations.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like opening a portfolio: hover reveals a little more information, buttons respond with a subtle press, and lightboxes preserve the feeling of handling a large-format plan.

### Animation
Use short opacity/translate entrances on section reveal, staggered by 50ms. Image cards lift by 4px on hover and brass rules expand from left to right. Avoid bouncy motion; keep easing calm and precise. Respect reduced motion.

### Typography System
Use Cormorant Garamond for display headlines, numeric facts, and section titles. Use DM Sans for body copy, navigation, labels, and controls. Headlines are high-contrast and often broken across two lines; body copy stays compact with a 1.65 line-height.

### Brand Essence
A private-garden address for families who want more room, more calm, and a better relationship with the city.
Personality: considered, grounded, quietly ambitious.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are confident but never loud. CTAs feel like invitations rather than pressure. Microcopy is concise, specific, and transparent.
Example lines: “Space that gives the day room to breathe.” / “Request the private floor plan set.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied Gillco wordmark as text styling rather than inventing a new wordmark. Pair it with a simple wing / unfolding-leaf emblem, used as a small architectural seal in the header and footer.

### Signature Brand Color
Meraqui Forest — #0E3B2E.

## Style Decisions
- Prefer warm cream surfaces over stark white.
- Use supplied project images for factual architecture and generated visuals only as atmosphere / supporting campaign imagery.
- Keep CTAs informative and RERA-conscious; prices remain On Request.
- No fabricated reviews or testimonials.
